Hello! I'm Angie Lee. I'm a 1st year PhD student at the University of California San Diego's [Department of Linguistics](https://linguistics.ucsd.edu/). My research focus is on computational phonology, and I'm advised by Prof. [Eric Baković](https://sites.google.com/ucsd.edu/ebakovic/). Before UCSD, I worked with Prof. [Scott Nelson](https://www.scott-nelson.net/) and completed my undergraduate studies at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/) where I received BS degrees in {Linguistics and Cognitive Science} and {Speech and Hearing Science}.
